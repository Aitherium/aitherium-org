# Code of Conduct

Aitherium and every project in the Aitherium organization adopt the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as our Code of Conduct. It applies in our repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, the Aitherium Discord, and at events where you represent a project.

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and
do not harass, demean, or exclude anyone.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues privately to **security@aitherium.com** with "Code of
Conduct" in the subject. Reports are read by the maintainers, kept confidential,
and answered. Maintainers who are part of a report step aside from handling it.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban, depending on the impact.
