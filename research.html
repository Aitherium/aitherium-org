Sponsor the research · Aitherium Foundation

A small lab that publishes its measurements.

We run small and 1-bit models on hardware people already own, and we publish what we measure: the wins, the ties, and the runs our own gates rejected. Sponsorship buys more of that work, on a schedule, with your name on the reports it pays for.

How the lab runs

Output, not headcount.

One founder directs many agent sessions that write, review and merge the platform. In October 2026 that meant 3,323 pull requests merged into our main repository (counted with GitHub search on 2026-11-13; the repository is private, the count method is not). The compute is a home lab, an RTX 5090 and a DGX Spark, plus GPUs rented by the hour for training, each rental entered in a ledger and released when the job ends.

Public, and checkable by anyone: 107 public repositories, packages on PyPI, and every post on the journal dated and linked to its run.

What we have not done, so you do not have to ask
Claim we do not makeWhere it stands
A fine-tune that beats its baseNot yet. Our best adapter tied its base inside the interval, so it was not promoted.
Pretraining, or training across many GPUsNone. Our training runs are LoRA on one GPU.
Our own CUDA kernelsNone. We wrote the WebGPU kernels for the browser; the native 1-bit kernels are PrismML's.
A published technical reportThe first is outlined: 1-bit inference in the browser, with the parity method and the variance.
