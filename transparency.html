Transparency

The record is public

The foundation is accountable to anyone who inspects it — not to its own press releases. Everything below is a link, not a claim.

Open source

The platform's source is published in public repositories — the core is source-available and free for personal use, and the ecosystem around it is permissively licensed. The foundation's own fleet runs on the same published code it hands to strangers — there is no "open for customers, closed for competitors" split.

Browse the public repositories →

Open weights

Models released by the foundation carry published weights. That is the difference between owning intelligence and renting it: with the weights, anyone can audit the model, fine-tune it, serve it, and keep it — forever, on their own hardware.

Why this page exists

Secrecy is the tax the public pays last

A non-profit that keeps its operations private is a trust without an audit. Every decision the foundation makes — what to open, what to release, how money moves — is a decision the record should show. Where the record does not exist yet, this page says so, rather than pretending.

Watch the record yourself

Follow the repositories and the status page — the record updates more often than this site does.
