What we believe

Principles

  1. 1

    Open by default

    If it does not have to be secret, it is published. Code, weights, documents, and the record of decisions — the default is openness, and secrecy must be justified case by case.

  2. 2

    Your hardware, your data, your agents

    Intelligence should run where the user chooses. The platform is designed to be self-hosted first — a user's data and agents belong to the user, on their machine.

  3. 3

    Anyone can become an operator

    You are not replaced by AI; you become its operator. A core educational mission: give every person the doctrine and tools to direct their own agents, not rent someone else's.

  4. 4

    Built in the open, from the inside

    The infrastructure that runs the foundation's own work is the same open platform we publish — we eat our own cooking, and the recipe is public.
